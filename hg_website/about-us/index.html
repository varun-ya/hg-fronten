Our Story

Built on three decades
of experience.

After spending over three decades in education, Vikram Ahuja saw the same pattern repeat. Students trying harder, yet not moving forward. Not because they lacked ability, but because they lacked the right guidance.

He understood something simple, yet powerful. When the match is right, everything changes. That belief led to the creation of HomeGuru.

Now, with his son Vansh Ahuja joining him, the vision has grown stronger and sharper. Bringing together decades of experience and a new-age approach to technology, they are building HomeGuru into a global learning platform designed for how students actually learn.
