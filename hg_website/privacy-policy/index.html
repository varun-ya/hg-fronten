This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the HomeGuru platform.

Effective Date: 1st April 2026 14 Sections
01

Introduction

HomeGuru (I) Private Limited ("HomeGuru", "we", "our", or "us") operates an online platform that connects students (learners) with independent tutors (service providers) for educational services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our platform, website, or services. By accessing or using HomeGuru, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

02

Information We Collect

To provide a seamless and effective experience, we collect certain types of information from users:

  • Personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, and location details
  • Usage-related data such as device information, IP address, browsing activity, and interaction with the platform
  • Communication data including chat interactions and call-related metadata (not recordings unless explicitly stated)
  • Payment-related information such as transaction details
  • Class-related data including session bookings, tutor interactions, and feedback provided
03

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is primarily used to ensure smooth functioning of the platform and to deliver a high-quality learning experience. We use your data to:

  • Match students with suitable tutors
  • Facilitate communication between users
  • Manage and schedule classes
  • Process payments securely
  • Improve our platform based on user behavior and feedback
  • Maintain platform safety, prevent fraud, and ensure compliance with legal obligations
04

Sharing of Information

HomeGuru does not sell your personal data to third parties.

  • We may share relevant information between students and tutors to enable classes and communication
  • We may share data with trusted third-party partners such as payment gateways, technology service providers, and analytics tools
  • We may be required to disclose information to legal authorities or government bodies in compliance with applicable laws
05

International Data Transfers

As part of operating a global platform, your information may be processed and stored in locations outside your country of residence.

By using HomeGuru, you consent to the transfer, storage, and processing of your data in such jurisdictions, subject to applicable data protection standards.

06

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

While we strive to ensure the highest level of data protection, no system can be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

07

Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Once the data is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymize it.

08

Your Rights

As a user, you have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal data.

If you wish to exercise these rights, you may contact us through the details provided below. We will take reasonable steps to process your request in accordance with applicable laws.

09

Cookies and Tracking

HomeGuru uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience, analyze platform usage, and improve our services.

By continuing to use the platform, you consent to the use of these technologies.

10

Children's Privacy

HomeGuru services may be used by students under the supervision of parents or guardians.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without appropriate supervision or consent.

11

Platform Role

HomeGuru acts as a technology platform that connects students with independent tutors.

Tutors are not employees of HomeGuru, and we do not take responsibility for the conduct, teaching methods, or outcomes of individual tutors. Our role is limited to facilitating the connection and enabling the learning experience.
